Words redact from the end of each line. The first word stays visible until every line has been reduced to it; the next step hides about half of each first word, and the final step leaves only its first letter. You can also use the left/right arrow keys when not typing.

Practice view

Redacted words appear as dark bars, keeping their original length and line breaks. After the other words are hidden, half of each first word remains briefly, then only its first letter. Hold “Peak” or anywhere on the script in Practice mode to reveal redacted text. Practice mode shows only this script; start Auto-scroll to move down the page hands-free, use the arrow keys to step through redactions and lines, or tap × to exit.
