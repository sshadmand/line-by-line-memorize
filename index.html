Line by Line

Practice a presentation by gradually removing its words.

Single-page memorization tool

Words redact from the end of each line. The first word stays visible until every line has been reduced to its first word; the final step redacts all words except the first letter of each line. You can also use the left/right arrow keys when not typing.

Practice view

Redacted words appear as dark bars, keeping their original length and line breaks. At the final step, the first letter of each line remains as a hint. Hold “Reveal answers” or anywhere on the script in Practice mode to peek. Practice mode shows only this script; use the arrow keys to step and follow the next line, or Escape to exit.
